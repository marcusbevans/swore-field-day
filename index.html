On the ground

What you'll actually see

A 3.3-acre corn maze with about 3 miles of path. Wendy Swore cuts it freehand with a weed whacker. Signs in the maze ask farm questions: a right answer leads on, a wrong one is a dead end. Ten emergency exits.

Three pumpkin patches, about 24,000 plants and 30 varieties this year. Regular pumpkins are 40¢ a pound. Minis are $2. Small field-trip size are $3. Specialty ones, like the Wolf, are priced each.

Maze and play area $8. Wagon alone $4. Haywagon Deal (both) $10. Family pass $47. Infants 1 and under free.

Play yard: straw maze, straw slides, wheat pool, Tire Mountain. Animals that roam the farm include chickens, geese, peacocks, and a lot of cats. Not a haunted maze.
